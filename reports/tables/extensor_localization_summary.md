# Wrist Extension Channel Localization (EMG)

We quantified whether wrist extension shows a compact cluster of highly active electrodes across trials by using the mean RMS amplitude per channel over the wrist‑extension window for each trial. For each trial, we identified the top‑k channels by mean RMS (k=5) and summarized where these channels occur on the 32‑channel ring.

**Findings (n=274 trials):**
- The most frequent high‑activation channels were clustered around channels 16–20, with the circular mean at ~channel 17.6.
- Across trials, the **median span** of the top‑k channels was **~5 channels**, indicating a compact cluster in most trials. The **mean span** was **~8.6 channels**, reflecting occasional broader spread.
- The most common channels were: **19, 18, 17, 16, 20, 15, 14, 21, 13** (descending frequency).

**Interpretation:**
Wrist extension produces a consistent, spatially compact region of higher activation on the electrode ring. The dominant channels cluster tightly in most trials, suggesting a repeatable spatial signature that could be used for orientation calibration or anatomical localization.
